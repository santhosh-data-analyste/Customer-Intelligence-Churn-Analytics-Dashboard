# 📊 Customer Intelligence & Churn Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📖 Problem Statement
Businesses often struggle to understand customer behavior, monitor key financial metrics, and identify factors leading to customer churn. Without a centralized analytics solution, decision-making becomes reactive rather than proactive.

This project aims to build an interactive **Customer Intelligence & Churn Analytics Dashboard** that enables organizations to track KPIs, analyze customer segmentation, and gain insights into financial and behavioral patterns for improved business decisions.

---

## 🔷 Overview
The **Customer Intelligence & Churn Dashboard** is a Power BI solution designed to transform raw customer and financial data into actionable insights. It provides a comprehensive view of customer distribution, income, loan patterns, and account behavior.

The dashboard helps stakeholders:
- Monitor financial KPIs  
- Understand customer segmentation  
- Analyze loan and income patterns  
- Improve customer retention strategies  

---

## 📊 Dataset Information
- **Dataset Type:** Banking / Customer Dataset  
- **Total Records:** ~2000 customers  
- **Key Attributes:**
  - Customer ID  
  - Gender  
  - City (Hyderabad, Bangalore, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Pune)  
  - Account Type (Current, Savings, Salary)  
  - Balance  
  - Loan Type (Home, Personal, Education, Car)  
  - Income  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard Development  
- **Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Transformation  
- **DAX** – KPI Calculations  
- **Data Visualization** – KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Donut Charts, Tables

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- 📌 Customer distribution by **Gender**  
- 📌 Customer analysis by **City**  
- 📌 Balance analysis by **Account Type**  
- 📌 Loan distribution by **Loan Type**  
- 📌 Income segmentation by **Customer Segment**  
- 📌 Interactive filters (City selection)  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Majority of customers are evenly distributed across gender  
- Cities like Hyderabad and Bangalore show higher customer concentration  
- Current accounts hold the highest balance share  
- Personal and education loans contribute significantly to total loan amount  
- Corporate and SME segments generate high income
